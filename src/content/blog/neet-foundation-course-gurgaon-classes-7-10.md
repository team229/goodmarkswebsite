<h2>What Does a NEET Foundation Course Do?</h2><br />

<h2>Introduction: Why Start Before Class 11?</h2><br />

<p>NEET-UG is one paper of 180 questions for 720 marks, with 4 marks for a correct answer and 1 deducted for a wrong one, as published by the National Testing Agency. Its syllabus, notified by the National Medical Commission, is based on NCERT Class 11 and 12 textbooks.</p><br />

<p>Class 11 builds directly on Class 9 and 10 basics. A student who never understood motion, atoms or the cell struggles when kinematics, mole concept and cell biology arrive at full speed. Foundation years close those gaps while pressure is low.</p><br />

<p>The format is changing too. The Education Minister has announced computer-based NEET-UG from 2027, so foundation students should get comfortable with on-screen tests.</p><br />

<h2>What Is a NEET Foundation Course in Gurgaon?</h2><br />

<p><strong>It is concept-first science coaching for Classes 7 to 10 that links the school syllabus to NEET-level thinking.</strong> It does not mean teaching Class 11 chapters to a Class 8 student. It means teaching current chapters more deeply, with application questions and reasoning that NEET later rewards.</p><br />

<h2>Expert Insights: What Makes a Foundation Year Work?</h2><br />

<p><strong>Depth beats speed.</strong> Rushing Class 11 content into Class 8 creates familiarity without understanding.</p><br />

<p><strong>Fix Physics early.</strong> Many medical aspirants postpone numericals, and weak calculation skills are much harder to repair in Class 11.</p><br />

<p><strong>Read NCERT line by line.</strong> Because the NEET syllabus is NCERT-based, careful reading of text and diagrams is a habit worth building in Class 9.</p><br />

<p><strong>Respect negative marking.</strong> Keep an error log that separates concept errors, calculation slips and misread questions.</p><br />

<p><strong>Practise on screen.</strong> With computer-based NEET announced, add timed online tests to paper worksheets.</p><br />
