<h2>What Is NEET UG Preparation?</h2><br />

<p>NEET UG preparation is the study of Physics, Chemistry and Biology (Botany and Zoology) for the medical entrance exam. The National Testing Agency conducts the exam, and its official portal is neet.nta.nic.in. Check that site for the current syllabus, schedule and eligibility rules.</p><br />

<p>Good NEET exam preparation builds NCERT mastery first. Then it adds question practice, speed and revision.</p><br />
