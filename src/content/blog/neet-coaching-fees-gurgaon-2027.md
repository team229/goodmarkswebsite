<p>Published NEET coaching fees in Gurgaon for the 2027 admission cycle run from ₹1.75 lakh to ₹4.60 lakh a year, according to Letsdiskuss, updated 4 October 2026. The spread tracks contact hours and batch size. The line item families miss is the repeat year. Confirm every figure with the institute.</p>

<p><strong>Key takeaways</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Three publishers describe Aakash's NEET fee three ways. Most of the gap is the word year.</li>
<li>One source prices NEET and JEE identically. Another puts NEET's ceiling higher. Neither proves NEET is dearer.</li>
<li>Divide by hours. ₹1.75 lakh over 300 hours is ₹583 an hour, more than ₹4.60 lakh over 1,050.</li>
</ul>

<p>Two rows describe one institute with two meanings of year. Letsdiskuss puts Aakash's NEET classroom fee at ₹1.75 lakh to ₹4.60 lakh <em>a year</em>. Collegedunia prices the two-year course at ₹4.70 lakh <em>total</em>, which is ₹2.35 lakh a year. That gap isn't a discount. Averaging gives ₹3.48 lakh, which nobody publishes. Ask which basis the quote uses.</p>

<p>A third source disagrees. <a href="https://www.aakash.ac.in/neet-coaching-centers">Aakash's own national NEET page</a>, read on 28 October 2026, answers with ₹1.50 lakh to ₹2.50 lakh a year offline and ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 online, with no Gurgaon qualifier. The manufacturer's figure undercuts the directory naming the same brand. Three publishers, three answers, none of them your invoice.</p>

<p>The classroom tables carry no exam year. Ask which exam year the batch you are quoted for sits in.</p>

<h2>Why does the same brand price NEET and JEE differently?</h2>

<p>Collegedunia lists Aakash's Gurgaon JEE and NEET classroom courses at identical prices: ₹4.70 lakh for two years, ₹2.40 lakh for one, ₹1.95 lakh for droppers. Letsdiskuss gives JEE ₹1.90 lakh to ₹3.41 lakh a year and NEET ₹1.75 lakh to ₹4.60 lakh, a wider band rather than a higher price. At Allen, one band covers both.</p>

<p><strong>Biology is two subjects.</strong> It is taught as Botany and Zoology, with separate teachers, test sections and revision. Two NCERT textbooks get read line by line, and that reading is paid time.</p>

<p><strong>The add-ons cost more at the same volume.</strong> Class 11 NEET material is ₹18,300 against ₹17,400 for JEE, and the NEET list holds 34 modules against 36. The Class 11 AITS is ₹16,154 against ₹7,326, for 50 tests against 51. More than twice the price for one test fewer.</p>

<li><strong>The test series.</strong> NEET packages run ₹12,999 to ₹25,153.</li>
<li><strong>The scholarship test.</strong> ANTHE carries a ₹100 application fee.</li>

<li><strong>Travel.</strong> No rupee figure; it depends on your address. A daily Manesar trip is paid in study hours.</li>

<p>Add two to a two-year bill: ₹4,70,000 plus ₹18,300 plus ₹16,154 is ₹5,04,454. The series covers Class 11 only, so treat that as the floor.</p>

<h2>What does a repeat year add to the bill?</h2>

<p>If the first attempt doesn't land, the second has a published price. Most families find out too late.</p>

<p>₹4,70,000 for the two-year course plus ₹1,95,000 for the dropper year is ₹6,65,000 for two attempts at one institute. That is ₹1,95,000 more than the two-year course alone, or 41% on top. Add both material sets, ₹18,300 and ₹18,470, and it reaches ₹7,01,770, if material is billed separately.</p>

<p>It need not be that number. The same page lists a seven-month online course for a 12-pass student at ₹70,000, so ₹4,70,000 plus ₹70,000 is ₹5,40,000. Online for a repeat year is real, and the arithmetic is why.</p>

<p>Then the part with no invoice. A repeat year costs a year, and a year is the one line no fee sheet can refund.</p>

<h2>Are the ANTHE and iACST scholarships real?</h2>

<p>Yes, and narrower than the banner. Collegedunia lists ANTHE at up to 100% on course fees offline and 80% online, read 28 October 2026. iACST runs up to 90% on tuition, and Letsdiskuss puts early enrolment before April or May at 5% to 20% off.</p>

<p>The bands decide whether the waiver means anything. For Class 11 to 12-pass, the ANTHE table puts a 100% tuition waiver at ranks 1 to 150 and 90% at ranks 151 to 300. From rank 301 the column reads N/A. The full waiver exists, for 150 students.</p>

<p>A 90% tuition waiver on ₹4.60 lakh leaves ₹46,000. It needs a top-300 national rank. A waiver makes a course cheaper, never better: hours, batch size and marked scripts stay as they were.</p>

<h2>What should be in writing before any money changes hands?</h2>

<p>Seven questions, each with the answer that should worry you.</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>How many total teaching hours, tests included?</strong> Worry at "the full syllabus".</li>
<li><strong>How many students are in this batch today?</strong> Worry at a cap, not a count.</li>
<li><strong>What is excluded from this number?</strong> Worry at "everything included" with no written list.</li>
<li><strong>Is the scholarship on tuition or the total?</strong> Worry if nobody can say.</li>
<li><strong>What is the refund policy, week by week?</strong> Worry at "case by case".</li>
<li><strong>How many doubt minutes per student per week, and is that time on the timetable?</strong> Worry at "anytime".</li>
<li><strong>If my child repeats, what does the second year cost?</strong> Worry at silence, more than at the amount.</li>
</ol>
