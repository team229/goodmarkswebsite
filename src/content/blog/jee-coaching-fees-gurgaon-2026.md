Those are directory figures, checked 26 October 2026, not quotes. The gap tracks seats per teacher, contact hours and what is bundled. Ask each institute for a written fee sheet before you compare.</p>

<h2>Why does one institute quote ₹45,000 a year and another ₹2.40 lakh a year for the same exam?</h2>

<p>Both prepare students for JEE Main and <a href="https://jeeadv.ac.in/">Advanced</a>. Neither number is dishonest, and three things move the price.</p>

<p><strong>Seats per teacher.</strong> A hall of fifty and a room of ten cost about the same per hour. Collegedunia lists Aakash's JEE batches at 50 to 55 students. Cap a batch at ten and the same teaching hour costs more per head.</p>

<p><strong>Contact hours.</strong> Few institutes publish this, and it decides whether two quotes compare at all. Take two invented courses: A at ₹45,000 with 300 contact hours, B at ₹2,40,000 with 600. That is ₹150 an hour against ₹400. The inputs are deliberately made up, because almost nobody publishes real ones. The method isn't. Ask both institutes for total teaching hours, tests included, and divide. If the answer is vague, that is your answer.</p>

<p><strong>Who teaches, and for how long.</strong> A brand fee funds recruitment, content teams and a building. A smaller fee funds one teacher's time. Ask for a name and a commitment; a change of teacher in Class 12 is expensive to absorb.</p>

<h2>What isn't in the quoted fee?</h2>

<p>This is where the published number stops matching the money leaving your account. Ask for each item in writing.</p>

<p><strong>Registration and admission fee.</strong> On <a href="https://collegedunia.com/institute/8724-aakash-institute-gurgaon">Collegedunia</a>, Aakash's registration fee is listed as never refundable, and a centre transfer costs ₹1,000.</p>

<p><strong>Printed material.</strong> ₹17,400 for a JEE study-material package in that listing. Ask whether the classroom fee already includes it, and whether the books are yours to keep.</p>

<p><strong>The test series.</strong> An all-India test is the closest thing to a national benchmark, and several institutes sell it separately. Aakash's JEE Main and Advanced test series run from ₹6,000 for a plain AITS to ₹19,014 for a ranker package with video solutions.</p>

<p><strong>GST and the invoice.</strong> Ask whether the quoted number includes GST and whether the invoice arrives in the institute's name. If an employer reimburses tuition, the format decides whether you see that money.</p>

<p><strong>Transport.</strong> Sector 95 or Manesar Sector 3 to Sector 14 is a round trip through NH-48 traffic, twice a day, for two years. Some of it comes out of study time.</p>

<p><strong>Board exam support.</strong> A JEE-only course leaves Class 11 and 12 boards to school. If boards slip, many families add CBSE tuition in the same year.</p>

<p><strong>The second year.</strong> The largest line item is not on the invoice. A dropper course at Aakash is listed at ₹1.95 lakh for one year. If the first attempt lands nowhere near the target, that is the real cost of choosing badly.</p>

<p>Two years at the top is ₹4.70 lakh before material and the commute.</p>

<h2>What counts as a small batch, and how do you prove it in one demo class?</h2>

<p>The word "small" appears on every Gurgaon ranking page we checked. Letsdiskuss puts city batches at 15 to 120 and calls one institute's 10 to 15 "genuinely rare". A word covering fifteen and a hundred and twenty carries no information.</p>

<p>The number that matters is doubt minutes per student per week. Thirty students who need eight minutes each need four hours a week beyond class. Ask where those hours sit on the timetable.</p>

<p>Then check in one visit, without announcing it.</p>

<ol><li>Count students at the twenty-minute mark, not at minute one.</li><li>Ask the teacher, in front of the batch, how many are present today and how many are enrolled.</li><li>Ask the student at the back how long they have been here, and what they scored last test.</li><li>Ask for a returned paper with the name blacked out. Look for written remarks, not ticks.</li><li>Ask who notices if your child misses three classes in a row.</li></ol>

<h2>What should the fee actually buy?</h2>

<p><strong>The same teacher, for two years.</strong> Continuity beats a famous name who appears at orientation. Ask who takes Physics in Class 12, and what happens if they leave. <a href="/blogs/iit-jee-coaching-gurgaon/">How to choose a JEE programme</a> covers the rest.</p>

<p><strong>Doubt time on the timetable.</strong> "Anytime" means after class, if the teacher is free. A weekly slot on the schedule is a commitment.</p>

<p><strong>Test review, not just tests.</strong> A rank without a marked script teaches nothing. You are buying the hour where someone shows your child the three questions that cost twenty marks.</p>

<p><strong>Parent reporting with a format.</strong> Ask how often, in what form, who writes it. Two lines beat a monthly scorecard.</p>

<h2>What should you ask before you pay?</h2>

<p>Eight questions, each with the answer that should worry you.</p>

<ol><li><strong>How many students are in this batch today?</strong> Worry if it is "around 30 to 40, we cap at 30". A cap that isn't the count is marketing.</li><li><strong>How many total teaching hours, including test discussion?</strong> Worry if there is no number. Without hours, two quotes cannot be compared.</li><li><strong>Who teaches Physics in Class 12, and will they still be here next year?</strong> Worry if you get "senior faculty" and "depends on the batch".</li><li><strong>When was the last test, and when were the papers returned?</strong> Worry if you see a score but no marked script. A test nobody marks measures the batch, not your child.</li><li><strong>How many doubt minutes per student per week, and is it scheduled?</strong> Worry if the answer is "anytime".</li><li><strong>What exactly is excluded from this fee?</strong> Worry if "nothing, all inclusive" arrives without a written list.</li><li><strong>What is the refund policy, week by week?</strong> Worry if you hear "no refunds" or "case by case".</li><li><strong>How many of last year's batch are repeating with you?</strong> Worry about "we don't track that" more than the number itself.</li></ol>

<p>One more settles it: ask for two parents' numbers.</p>

<h2>When is a smaller programme the better financial decision?</h2>

<p>Price is not cost. Four cases where the cheaper fee wins.</p>

<p><strong>One weak subject, not a weak syllabus.</strong> Paying ₹2.40 lakh to re-run two years because rotational motion never clicked is an expensive fix. Two focused hours a week can do more.</p>

<p><strong>A dropper with a diagnosed gap.</strong> Dropper courses are cheaper: ₹1.95 lakh against ₹4.70 lakh in the Aakash listings. Check the hours, because the same syllabus is compressed into fewer months.</p>

<p><strong>A long commute.</strong> From Sectors 88 to 99 or Manesar 1 to 5, a daily trip to Sector 14 is paid in study time, not just fuel.</p>

<p><strong>A possible change of stream.</strong> Ask what happens if your child moves from JEE to NEET in the first term. The programmes and material differ, and the refund clock may not restart. Better to know in June than December.</p>

<p>A small batch can also mean nobody joined, or one teacher covers all three subjects. Ask how many subjects each faculty member handles.</p>

<h2>How should you read a scholarship or early-enrolment discount?</h2>

<p>The sticker and the paid price differ. Aakash's ANTHE offers up to 100% off tuition on the offline exam, up to 80% online; its iACST offers up to 90%. Letsdiskuss notes that enrolling before April or May takes another 5 to 20% off.</p>

<p>Three checks first. Does the waiver cover tuition only, since registration, material and test series usually sit outside it? Is it conditional on a marks threshold? Does it stack with the early-enrolment discount?</p>

<p>Then do the arithmetic. Thirty per cent off ₹3.41 lakh is about ₹2.39 lakh, still more than five times the ₹45,000 option. A discount says nothing about what the extra buys. And a discount expiring tomorrow is a sales device; published fees do not move 20% overnight.</p>
