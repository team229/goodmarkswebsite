# Good Marks Classes

> Good Marks Classes is a coaching institute in Gurgaon and Manesar, Haryana, India, founded by Sunil Gola Sir (DTU alumnus). It offers IIT-JEE (Main & Advanced) coaching, NEET-UG coaching, and CBSE tuitions for Classes 8–12, plus subject-wise tuition, home tutoring, olympiad/CUET/NDA entrance prep, and doubt-clearing sessions — delivered offline, online, hybrid, and at students' homes. Batches are capped at 10 students online and 30 offline.

## Programs

- [IIT-JEE Coaching](https://www.goodmarksclasses.com/courses/iit): 2-year and 1-year regular programmes for JEE Main & Advanced, BITSAT, KVPY.
- [NEET-UG Coaching](https://www.goodmarksclasses.com/courses/neet): 2-year and 1-year regular programmes for NEET & AIIMS.
- [CBSE Tuitions](https://www.goodmarksclasses.com/courses/cbse): Tuition for Classes 8–12 covering Physics, Chemistry, Maths, and Biology.
- [Physics by Sunil Gola](https://www.goodmarksclasses.com/physics-classes-sunil-gola): Physics classes taught personally by the founder, for CBSE, JEE & NEET.
- [Home Tuition](https://www.goodmarksclasses.com/home-tuition): One-to-one tutoring at the student's home in Gurgaon.
- [Olympiad & Entrance Coaching](https://www.goodmarksclasses.com/olympiad): CUET, NDA, and Science/Maths Olympiad (NSO, IMO) preparation.
- [Doubt Sessions](https://www.goodmarksclasses.com/doubt-sessions): One-to-one doubt-clearing, online and offline, across all subjects.
