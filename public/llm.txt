# Good Marks Classes

> Good Marks Classes is a coaching institute in Gurgaon and Manesar, Haryana, offering CBSE tuition, subject classes, IIT-JEE and NEET-UG programmes, foundation coaching, home tuition, Olympiad and entrance-exam preparation, and doubt-clearing sessions.

## About

Good Marks Classes presents small-group academic coaching led by Sunil Gola Sir. Its public programmes cover CBSE Classes 8–12, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology, IIT-JEE, NEET-UG, foundation preparation, home tuition, Olympiad and entrance exams, and one-to-one doubt sessions. The site lists a Gurgaon centre and serves students from sectors 78–99 in Gurgaon and sectors 1–5 in Manesar.
